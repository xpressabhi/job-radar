How this list is built

  • Companies: a hand-vetted list; each employer must clear ₹20L base pay generally and ₹50L base at senior level in India. Company bands are recorded with sources in the repo and refined by pay data observed in real postings.
  • Roles: senior+ engineering titles only (Senior / Staff / Principal / Lead / Architect / Director / EM / VP). Where pay is published, the band's lower bound must clear ₹50L base; equity and bonus never count. Where pay is not published, the company's vetted senior band applies.
  • Geography: India-located, remote-India, and unrestricted-remote roles (the latter flagged "verify eligibility"). Roles restricted to other regions are excluded.
  • Freshness: crawled daily from public ATS boards. A role leaves the active list after two consecutive successful crawls without it, and lives on in the archive. Failed crawls never remove anything.
